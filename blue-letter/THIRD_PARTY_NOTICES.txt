## Transformers.js 4.3.0 and Whisper (not bundled)

Used only when someone starts 🎙 聞き取って合わせる (listening to the song to time the lyrics). They are downloaded
at that moment and run in the browser; the song and lyrics are never sent anywhere.

- Transformers.js (https://github.com/huggingface/transformers.js), loaded from jsDelivr — Apache License 2.0.
- Whisper speech-recognition models by OpenAI (https://github.com/openai/whisper) — MIT License — in the ONNX
  conversions published on Hugging Face: onnx-community/whisper-large-v3-turbo(_timestamped) and
  onnx-community/whisper-small(_timestamped).
